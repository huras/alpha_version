World Base Information

- Arc:
	* An exerpt of plot that have begining, middle and conclusion.
	* Like game expansions, its components are connected with the already existing content in the story.
	The components of an arc are:
		0 - Current State
			* The current life state of the protagonist before living the Arc that will be generated.
		1 - Spoil / Moral Lesson / Climax Event
			* The purpose of that arc in the path of the character, what spoils may it give to him (or not).
			* The AI will generate a moral meaning for the arc
		2 - Motif (AI)
			* Motif is generated according to spoil defined by the [Spoil / Moral Lesson / Climax Event] mix
		3 - Character Organizations
			- Prompts: 
				- Define at least 5 greater organizations and destaque the biggest and second biggest ones.
				- Define 3 much smaller but still important organizations
				- Define 12 important families
			* Political, religious and industrial organizaitons that will be used to tell the Arc
		4 - Characters
			- Prompts:
				- Define the higher hierarchies of the organizatinons
				- Define characters not in the organizations but affected by them (positive or negavively)
				- Define characters not in the organizations but liked to it
			* The Characters are created only with a backstory and current motivation related to it.
			* Characters evolve from its initial state though the events.
			* Events cause update and increment in the characters profiles.
		5 - Locations (places, cities, countries, buildings, forests, rivers, cave, mountains, etc...)
			* Locations are created 3rd because the locations will be generated form the characters backstory.
			* Locations can change though events.
			- Creation source:
				- Organizations descriptions
				- Characters backstories
		6 - Events
			* Events display:
				- Text Dialog
					* The story only evolves through description of events:
						- Narration
						- Encounter
							- Observing other people interacting
							- Conversation	
						- Weather
						- Surroundings
						- Protagonist Inner thinking
				- Location transition (In Scene context)
				- Battles
				- Item give/take

- Base Arc: The story backbone and theme. The AI will generate or suggest sub arcs to compose the main arc.
	* Based on a upcoming "world" crisis to which the current society is mostly oblivious.
	- Examples
		* Main arc of avatar: learn the 4 elements to defeat the fire lord to end the war and restore balance while.
